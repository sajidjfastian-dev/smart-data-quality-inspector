# FinVibe judging readiness assessment

**Assessment date:** 17 November 2026. **Scope:** repository and supplied judging slide; targeted synthetic regression tests; official CBUAE sources. No application changes, production deployment, external model calls, or sensitive/real data access were performed. This is not a predicted score, a security audit, or a regulatory compliance assessment.

The supplied [judging slide](attached_assets/image_1794899896256.png) identifies six criteria, scored by judges from 1–5 and weighted as shown below. The slide is the source for the rubric; no official online copy or more detailed marking guidance was verified. The app is a demonstrable prototype for synthetic files, not yet an evidenced real-data pilot. A loading skeleton was captured in the preview, not a completed independent first-time walkthrough; no usability conclusion should be drawn from that capture.

**Merged-branch update:** the app also includes a Data insights tab for numeric, category, date and relationship exploration ([implementation](data_insights.py)). Some exploratory views use an evenly spaced sample of at most 100,000 rows; quality checks still scan the complete file. These additional views do not by themselves establish business impact or real-data readiness.

**Evidence labels:** **Observed** = present in inspected code or documentation, with test results identified separately. **Gap** = an absent capability or missing artifact established in the reviewed material. **Unverified** = may exist outside this review, but evidence was not available. A capability or passing test is not proof of business benefit, security, or production operation.

## Business impact: establish a defensible baseline

**Candidate problem, not yet validated:** an analyst manually identifies and resolves quality defects in incoming tabular data before using it for reporting; repetitive inspection and manual handoffs may delay the report and create rework. A business owner must confirm the actual workflow and affected population before using this claim.

**Relevant official priority:** CBUAE's 25 April 2024 [SupTech and Enterprise Data Management announcement](https://www.centralbank.ae/media/z0xbwybu/the-cbuae-enhances-suptech-initiative-as-part-of-its-financial-infrastructure-transformation-programme-en.pdf) describes strengthening data integrity and using analytics, automation and AI-driven decision-making. Improving an upstream data-quality review could be *relevant* to that stated priority; the announcement does not establish this tool's suitability, use by CBUAE, endorsement, or any regulatory outcome.

**Current baseline that can be reproduced:** the quick sample scan reports 10 findings in 400 cells and the test asserts 3 resolved and 7 remaining after the default fixes; the original remains unchanged. These are synthetic test outcomes, not defect rates, saved hours, prevented losses, or real-world accuracy. The health score counts findings relative to cells; a cell can have multiple findings and a duplicate is a row-level finding, so it is not a validated percentage of correct data.

**Measurement plan before calculating benefits:**

1. Business and data owners define the unit of work (for example, one reporting extract), its period, frequency and scope; obtain approved records or a representative de-identified sample and a labeled reference. Record provenance, data limitations and authorization.
2. Observe the **current method** on matched work: number of extracts per period, per-extract analyst time (including checking and correction), quality defects found and missed, and downstream rework. Record labor cost per hour and source/date, and keep setup and review time.
3. Compare with app-assisted work on a matched set, including upload, configuration, validation, fixes, manual review and export. Independently verify residual errors and any newly introduced errors. Report sample size, variation and selection bias.
4. Only after measurement, compute: **net hours per period = volume × (baseline minutes per extract − app-assisted minutes per extract) ÷ 60 − incremental setup/support hours**; **net monetary benefit = net hours × approved loaded hourly cost − incremental non-labor costs**. Avoid counting the same avoided rework in both time and cost. If relevant, separately measure reporting delays or error impacts rather than adding unsubstantiated loss-avoidance.
5. Publish each observed input, its source, period and uncertainty; show a range or sensitivity using measured variation, and explicitly mark extrapolation assumptions. No quantified savings or ROI can currently be supported.

## Innovation and comparison

Compare the same approved, labeled extracts and tasks across: (1) spreadsheets/manual filters, (2) existing rules/scripts or a deployed data-quality tool if available, (3) this app with local rules only, and (4) the same app with the configured model. Record capabilities actually available to the problem owner, total steps and time, missed/false findings, corrections requiring review, costs and governance constraints. A possible differentiator is an integrated inspect → explain → review → export workflow, **not a proven novel invention**. Ask the problem owner which alternative is in use and document a concrete task the combination improves; do not imply cross-file referential checks from shared sample keys.

## AI assessment and evaluation protocol

**Observed comparison, not measured superiority:** rule-only mode uses deterministic, supported question patterns and the current scan; it is limited to the enabled checks and matched queries. Model mode sends the dataset, profile, finding counts, recent conversation and question to a user-configured endpoint and may address questions beyond those patterns. No evidence yet shows it answers accurately or adds useful judgment.

1. With business/data owners, construct a versioned synthetic or authorized de-identified benchmark: labeled missing/invalid/duplicate findings, valid outliers, ambiguous dates, misleading column names, disabled checks, questions requiring calculations and questions the data cannot answer. Include prompts requiring domain judgment that the local assistant cannot provide. An independent reviewer supplies expected answers and grounds them in data.
2. Run identical questions against local rules and an approved fixed model/provider/version and settings, using the same data, enabled checks and no contaminating conversation history; repeat model trials to measure variation. Retain sanitized prompts, outputs and evaluation protocol. Mark unsupported local answers explicitly rather than treating silence as a correct answer.
3. Blind-review numerical correctness, finding precision/recall where reference labels exist, appropriate abstention, grounded explanation and unsafe or invented recommendations. Report denominators, failure examples and uncertainty for each mode. Separately track time and human corrections; decide acceptance thresholds with the problem owner **before** the run. AI adds value only if it improves relevant tasks without unacceptable errors or privacy risk.
4. Exercise synthetic prompt-injection cells and user requests to override instructions, invent results, reveal unrelated data, or claim edits; test malformed responses, timeouts and oversized requests. Record failures rather than assuming the system prompt prevents them. Check outputs against deterministic calculations and require human approval for decisions.
5. Inspect actual outbound payloads with a controlled test endpoint: sharing off must cause no model request; on must disclose and send the documented fields; clearing settings and changing dataset/endpoint should be tested. Confirm authorization and the provider's data processing, retention, region and access terms before any real data; default-off consent is not a substitute for institutional approval. Model endpoint currently allows HTTP and does not implement the data API's public-HTTPS/pinned-IP/redirect restrictions; assess plaintext, internal-address and redirect risks before any exposed pilot. Consent is a session checkbox, not demonstrably bound to a particular data/endpoint combination. There is no observed automatic redaction or model-answer validator.

## First-time walkthrough to run

Recruit intended first-time users, including the problem owner, without coaching; record consent and avoid real sensitive data until authorized. Give each participant the same task: load the quick sample, identify duplicate and missing issues, inspect a finding, apply selected fixes, verify the original versus fixed copy and export, then ask the local assistant what remains. Also ask them to locate and explain model-sharing consent without activating it. Record completion, elapsed time, clicks, wrong turns, errors, requests for help and whether they correctly understand the health score and manual-review limits. Repeat on representative desktop and smaller viewport, keyboard navigation and assistive technology where relevant. Compare with the existing workflow and set success criteria with the owner in advance. Capture anonymized observations, remediation and a repeat run; the current synthetic tests are not a substitute.

## Prioritized next steps and dependencies

1. **Validate the problem and establish an authorized baseline** (25%; also supports deployment and innovation). Business/data owners choose the workflow and data, approve access and agree on success measures. Until then, do not calculate or claim savings or run a real-data trial.
2. **Test model value and privacy against rules** (20%; depends on approved synthetic/de-identified benchmark and, for provider calls, approved provider). Evaluate accuracy, abstention, adversarial behavior, consent and payloads; address the model endpoint's weaker network protections before exposing a pilot.
3. **Define a secure pilot and run representative validation** (15%; depends on data approval and technical/security ownership). Establish access, audit/monitoring, operating responsibilities, load limits and recovery; verify end-to-end with authorized representative data.
4. **Run first-time tasks and compare alternatives** (15% + 15%; depends on clear tasks and matched baseline). Quantify self-service success and document a genuine workflow difference, then retest any remediation.
5. **Assemble cross-functional evidence and owner-led demo** (10%; starts immediately, completes after the above). Capture business, data and technology decisions and the owner's approval without inventing participation.

Other identified concerns include API pagination and OAuth, the visible findings count after filtering, and searches with special characters. Their relevance depends on the pilot workflow; check current project task status before planning additional work.
